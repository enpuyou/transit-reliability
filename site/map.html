Live bus map

Each dot is a King County Metro bus, as it last reported itself. The map updates by itself every 10 seconds (buses glide to their new position); you never need to refresh. Thin lines are the routes; select a route to see all of its stops (bigger rings are timetable stops). Each bus is an arrow pointing the way it is heading; click a bus to highlight its whole route, with arrows along the line showing which way traffic flows in each direction. This map shows where buses are; it does not say how late they are (the live feed carries no lateness). Lateness and history come from the daily numbers on the pipeline page.
