Live bus map

Each dot is a King County Metro bus, shown where it last reported itself (usually about a minute ago, so the bus has moved on a little). The map updates by itself every 10 seconds (buses glide to their new position); you never need to refresh. Thin lines are the routes; choose a route to see its buses and all of its stops (small rings; a filled dark ring is a timetable stop, where the schedule holds buses to a set time, and a hollow one is any other stop, whose scheduled time is calculated in between; click a stop to see when the next buses are predicted to arrive. Each bus is a coloured dot with a faint beam in front of it showing the way it is heading; click a bus to highlight its whole route, with arrows along the line showing which way traffic flows in each direction. This map shows where buses are; it does not say how late they are (the live feed carries no lateness). Lateness and history come from the daily numbers on the pipeline page.
