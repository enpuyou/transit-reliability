## What was built since 2026-10-20 (all in decision 0025 with measurements)

- **Live map** (`site/map.html`): the agency's bus positions every 10 s through our relay Worker; route lines,
  stops, a direction picker; each bus a coloured dot with a beam showing its heading.
- **Position estimate:** reports reach the map a median 57 s old. Each bus is drawn where it probably is now
  ("mixed, halfway": recent speed and the timetable's pace), with the real report as a hollow circle and its
  age. Replay on 240 trips: typical error 120 m against 253 m for the raw report. The page checks itself live.
- **Skips, cancellations, off-route buses:** read from the agency's own marks; off-route buses greyed.
- **History in the popups:** how late each exact trip usually is at each stop (`data/trip_history/`), and how
  late the route usually is at each timetable stop by time of day (`data/stop_history.json`), both from the
  daily job.
- **Trip memory** (a Durable Object in the relay, every 30 s): remembers trips that finished, so the map can
  say "a bus should be waiting at the start", "still on its previous trip", or "the previous trip never showed
  up". Up continuously since 2026-10-22 00:28 UTC.
- **Traffic on the chosen route only:** TomTom's speed for the roads of that route, clipped to the route.
- **Hourly check fix** (2026-10-21): weather freshness now reads the hourly landed files, not the daily table.
- **Daily check fix** (2026-10-22): silver weather now drops repeated readings inside a batch (the daily job
  failed on 2026-10-22 at its check step; data was built, the website refresh and map history were skipped).
